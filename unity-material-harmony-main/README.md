# Unified Material Hub

MASTER PROMPT — SIH-LEVEL NATIONAL UNIFIED MATERIAL MASTER PLATFORM

ROLE

Act as a Principal Enterprise Product Architect, Senior Frontend Engineer, UX Architect and Government/CPSE Enterprise Software Designer with 20+ years of experience.

Build a fully interactive, production-style frontend prototype for the Smart India Hackathon problem:

“AI-Driven Standardization and Harmonization of Material Codes Across CPSEs”

The objective is NOT to create a conceptual demo, static website, or generic AI dashboard.

Build a convincing SIH-level working prototype that looks and behaves like a real enterprise platform that could realistically be deployed across Indian CPSEs such as Oil & Gas, Power, Steel, Mining and Heavy Engineering organizations.

1. CORE PRODUCT CONCEPT

Create a platform called:

National Unified Material Master (NUMM)

Tagline:

One Nation • One Material Code

The platform acts as a centralized material-master harmonization and governance system connecting multiple CPSEs.

The prototype must demonstrate how different CPSEs can have:

Different material codes

Different descriptions

Different units

Different specifications

Different classifications

Different naming conventions

Duplicate materials

Near-duplicate materials

Functionally equivalent materials

…and how the platform can identify, standardize, compare, map and govern them under a Common National Material Code.

The prototype should preserve complete traceability to the original CPSE material codes.
